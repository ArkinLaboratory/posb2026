# Board notes

Print this and carry it. The deck records what is *projected*; this records what
gets *written*.

**This session is not a board session.** Every derivation is a split-surface
step run with the picture advancing beside the algebra. The board carries the
**ledger** — the lines the runs keep pointing back at, an hour after they were
written — and the **axes**, drawn once and never wiped.

**Conventions.** **ASK** — put it to the room before writing, and the answer to
expect. **POINT** — the moments you turn and point at the board rather than the
screen. **IF ASKED** — the question that always comes.

## The board plan

Screen down in the **centre**, a **left wing** and a **right wing**.

**LEFT WING — the ledger. Five lines, written as they are derived, never wiped.**

**RIGHT WING — the $(u, v)$ axes at minute 12, and the two vote tallies above

## Left wing, line 0 — minute 0, during the retrieval

$$\frac{du}{dt} = \frac{a}{1+v^{n}} - u \qquad\qquad \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{a}{1+u^{n}} - v$$

Up before anyone writes anything, and up all period.

## Left wing, line 1 — minute 12, the last step of Run 0

$$a = \frac{\alpha}{(\gamma+\mu)\,K}$$

**Say the symbol collision once, here.** On this line $\gamma + \mu$ is
removal, from session 5, and the Hill coefficient is $n$, from session 4.
Gardner's paper calls the two Hill coefficients $\beta$ and $\gamma$; the
handout uses his letters, and there is no removal rate in it. Never put his
$\gamma$ and our $\gamma+\mu$ in one equation.

**ASK:** *three things in the lab change $a$ — name them.* Promoter (up),
degradation tag (down), operator affinity (up, through $K$). All three enter
through one number.

## Left wing, line 2 — minute 19, the end of Run 1

$$u = \frac{a}{1+v^{n}} \qquad\qquad v = \frac{a}{1+u^{n}}$$

Directly under line 0, labelled *$du/dt = 0$* and *$dv/dt = 0$*.

**ASK before writing:** *on the first curve, which variable is still?* Expect
"both". The answer is $u$ only — and that is the whole idea of a nullcline.

## Left wing, line 3 — minute 50, the last step of Run 3b

$$J = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & -g \\ -g & -1 \end{pmatrix}
\qquad \lambda = -1 \pm g
\qquad g \equiv \frac{n\,x^{n+1}}{a}$$

**POINT at line 2 as you write $g$:** it came from differentiating the first
nullcline and using the second to clear the square.

**ASK:** *what is $g$ in words?* A loop gain — how much of a nudge in $v$ shows
up in the rate of $u$, at the fixed point. Session 7's positive autoregulation
had the same condition as a slope.

**IF ASKED** *why is $J$ symmetric?* — because both removal rates were scaled to
1 and the point is on the diagonal. Tag one repressor and neither holds. That
is item 4 of the handout, and Thursday.
