$$s_c = 5.4 \iff 39\ \mu\text{M IPTG} \qquad (K = 2.96\times10^{-5}\ \text{M},\ \eta = 2.0)$$

**POINT at the 15.6 and the 156 as you write it.** Nothing about the circuit
was changed to get this number; it is the legend's own parameters and a count

**ASK:** *what did the IPTG do to the picture?* One curve stretched, the other
did not move. If the room says "both moved", go back one step.

high state exists at zero IPTG; it always did. Nothing pulls the cell off a

## Left wing, line 2 — minute 60, after the ConcepTest

$$\text{threshold} \leftarrow \alpha, \beta, \gamma \qquad\qquad \text{switching time} \leftarrow \text{removal}$$

**ASK before writing the right half:** *what was measured in Fig. 6 — a
threshold or a time?* A time. Three to four hours to begin and done by six going
up; thirty-five minutes down — and the difference is dilution against heat
destruction.

**IF ASKED** *does a tag on one repressor change the threshold too?* — yes.
In scaled units removal sits inside $\alpha$, and the Jacobian is no longer
symmetric. That is item 4 of Tuesday's handout and PS4 Q5.
